# Equipt-Project-Repository

Project Title: Equipt

Team Members: Beatriz Barrios, Ben Krawiec, Jake Fratzke, Makayla Mires

•	The Problem: Consumers and DIY enthusiasts frequently face a steep financial barrier when undertaking home improvement, hobby, or specialized projects because they must purchase expensive tools they may only use once. Conversely, tool owners have thousands of dollars in valuable assets sitting idle in garages and sheds, taking up physical space and depreciating without generating any return on investment.

•	Why It Matters & Who is Affected: This mismatch affects suburban homeowners, renters, budget-conscious DIYers, and local hobbyists. On an individual level, it leads to unnecessary consumer debt and cluttered homes. On a broader scale, it contributes to environmental waste through the over-manufacturing of underutilized products.

•	The Solution: Equipt is a peer-to-peer equipment rental web application that bridges this gap. By creating a secure community marketplace, tool owners can monetize their idle assets while renters gain affordable, on-demand access to the tools they need. The platform streamlines the process with structured booking, and user reviews, transforming underused items into community-shared value.
